# Other human Hebrew One Piece translations: sources checked

Scope: everything except the Netflix live-action subtitles, the official One Pace Hebrew files, Hebrew Wikipedia and onepiece-nakama.com. Checked 2026-11-07. Downloads are in `.../scratchpad/attacks/other-dl/`. The entries are in `.../scratchpad/attacks/other.json`.

## Found (produced entries)
- **FOUND: OpenSubtitles ep. 165 (Shay1980, 2026, human).** https://www.opensubtitles.org/en/subtitles/13886212. Japanese attack names with glosses («גומו גומו נו פוּסן!» plus a separate «[פוּסן = בלון]» cue; «גומו גומו נו גאטלינג!!»), «מנטרה», «רומח הלהבות». The translator adds bracketed notes for Japanese wordplay. Only one episode exists. File: `scratchpad/osep/13886212/`.
- **FOUND (machine, negative example): OpenSubtitles ep. 001.** https://www.opensubtitles.org/en/subtitles/9729093. «מסטיק-מסטיק... אֶקְדָח!», «...רָקֵטָה!», «פרי המסטיק». Gum is translated as chewing gum, with dictionary niqqud.
- **FOUND (partial): official Jetix Hebrew dub (Elrom 2006, eps 1–52).** The only text-readable clip is YouTube `x5F8Zji0D-M` ("Omri's Stash", ep. 3 scene). Its Hebrew auto-captions give Zoro's Santōryū line as «אני נלחם בשלוש חרבות» (descriptive). That text comes from speech recognition, not a transcript. No Gum-Gum shout appears in the clip. Captions are at `other-dl/yt/x5F8Zji0D-M.iw-orig.vtt`.
  - The uploader's pinned comment links a Google Drive folder of surviving full dub episodes: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1ORjADnfmt2iZ6_konGyDU_hSdZDm5nHO. I did **not** download it: it is a recording of a pay-TV channel and audio only. A human could listen to ep. 1 (Pistol/Rocket), ep. 8 (Bazooka), and the Kuro and Arlong fights to get the dub's attack names. This is the best untapped lead.
  - The archive.org item for ep. 21, `one-piece-s-1-e-21-576p`, is now dark (removed).
- **FOUND: Hebrew One Piece Fandom wiki** (https://onepiece.fandom.com/he/, 115 articles, fan-written, partly machine-translated).
  - Gear Second / Third «הילוך שני / שלישי», «טכניקת שלושת החרבות», «טכניקת ששת החרבות», «רוקושיקי», «צעדי ירח», «סיימי קיקן», «כדור ראמבל».
  - Haki forms: «האקי הקשחה / חיזיון / מלכים» alongside the transliterations.
  - There is no attack list; Luffy's "powers and abilities" subpage is empty. Dump: `other-dl/hefandom/all.txt`.
- **FOUND (fan usage only): FXP One Piece forum** (https://www.fxp.co.il/forumdisplay.php?f=3731).
  - Fans write attacks in Japanese («גומו גומו נו באזוקה», 2015) or mixed Hebrew and Latin letters ("גומו גומו נווווווו JETO PISTOL…", 2021).
  - Thread IDs could not be recovered. WebFetch returns 403; Exa returns text without links.
- **FOUND: Toktok (Israeli YouTube fan translator).** Clip title «…גלקסי אימפקט – מתורגם לעברית» (Garp, ep. 1114), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7vzv21ABUtk. Other clips are hardsubbed and only the titles were read.
